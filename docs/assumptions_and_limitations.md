# Assumptions and Limitations

## Overview
This document summarizes the core assumptions and limits of the PowerUPCMP Proof of Concept (PoC).

## Synthetic vs. Public Evidence
All evidence currently generated by the simulator is purely **synthetic**. No proprietary, internal, or production real-world data was used to fit or test the models in the repository. Public references (e.g. baseline equations from literature) were utilized to shape the qualitative behavior of the simulator, but parameters remain uncalibrated to any physical tool.

## Key Modeling Assumptions (with Provenance)
1. **Lumped Parameter Spatial Proxy**: Wafer spatial effects are modeled as a lumped-parameter equivalent (A-019). The PoC does not resolve 2D wafer topology.
2. **Deterministic Process Flow**: The recipe structure follows a strict timing loop with fixed delays for `DRESS` and `POLISH` (A-012).
3. **Instantaneous Mode Switching**: Although there is hysteresis in threshold detection, the physical effect of a mode switch (e.g. from POLISH to SAFE_HOLD) is instantaneous in the ODEs.

## Operational Limitations
- The predictive controller has only been evaluated under synthetic fault injections (pump trip, voltage sag, etc.) and does not reflect actual fault transition distributions.
- End-to-end latency measurements (< 50ms) are tied strictly to local workstation execution, and do not guarantee industrial PLC/edge performance.
