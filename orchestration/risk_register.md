# Risk register

Scales: likelihood and impact are `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, or `HIGH`. Status is `OPEN`, `WATCH`, `MITIGATED`, or `CLOSED`. The sole engineering agent owns monitoring unless a user decision is named.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Status | Trigger / evidence | Mitigation and contingency |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| R-001 | PHM 2016 CMP source or licence cannot be fully verified. | MEDIUM | HIGH | WATCH | The local archive, official challenge page, filenames, raw schema, and checksums are verified, but no separate dataset licence statement was found. | Limit use to the recorded user-authorized local study; do not redistribute or assert a licence; retain exact source bytes/checksums. |
| R-002 | Public-data splitting or preprocessing leaks wafer, trace, time, label, or holdout information. | MEDIUM | HIGH | WATCH | R2 isolates official splits and labels, adds whole-wafer random/chronological splits and fit-scope audits; WP09 fitted transforms could still violate the contract. | Enforce the R2 predictor-column and fit-group manifests in every model pipeline; reject any transform/model artifact whose fit groups overlap official holdouts. |
| R-003 | Utility-to-CMP coupling is weakly identifiable and results merely encode chosen synthetic coefficients or topology. | HIGH | HIGH | WATCH | WP10 shows exact zero effect for no connection/zero link and a 3.19% later-MRR change for the nominal dressing link; global/mismatch results materially depend on link strength and thresholds, and no paired real utility/CMP data exist. | Keep no-connection and zero-link cases in every evaluation; retain complete coefficient provenance and preregistered ranges; report topology/global/local/mismatch sensitivity; never select coupling from controller performance; frame conclusions as simulation-only. |
| R-004 | Lumped electrical/hydraulic dynamics use invalid timescales or unstable integration. | MEDIUM | HIGH | WATCH | R3 nominal/limiting cases are bounded, mass/energy bookkeeping is explicit, and 20/10/5/2.5/1 ms errors decrease against a 0.5 ms reference; CMP coupling and mismatch may introduce new timescales. | Retain the 10 ms bound and invariant stops; repeat convergence after CMP/runtime integration and under parameter mismatch before efficacy evaluation. |
| R-005 | Latent simulator truth leaks into online predictors, attribution, controller, or safety filter. | MEDIUM | HIGH | WATCH | R4 queues observations by arrival and validates source boundaries, but predictors/controllers are not yet implemented and could bypass the observation API. | Enforce ObservationRecord-only online APIs and arrival cutoffs in WP12--WP17; dependency tests; post-decision-only truth joins; reject legacy observations without audited source time. |
| R-006 | Prediction intervals or excursion probabilities are poorly calibrated under scenario shift. | HIGH | HIGH | OPEN | Coverage failure, confident misses, poor reliability diagram, compound-scenario degradation. | Dedicated calibration groups; coverage and calibration metrics; high-uncertainty safety rejection/hold; unseen-scenario tests. |
| R-007 | Predictive control appears better because controller runs use different disturbances or random corruptions. | MEDIUM | HIGH | OPEN | Run manifests differ outside controller identity; seeds consumed conditionally. | Named child seeds; paired manifests; pre-generated exogenous traces; equality assertions; paired metrics. |
| R-008 | The controller exploits simulator/model mismatch or unsafe action combinations. | MEDIUM | HIGH | OPEN | Large action, chattering, envelope violation, sensitivity collapse, unrealistic recovery. | Bounded actions and slew; independent filter; hysteresis/dwell; mismatch robustness; hold fallback; audit all action paths. |
| R-009 | Safety filter is coupled to the predictive controller and fails independently. | LOW | HIGH | OPEN | Safety module imports controller internals, trusts proposal metadata, or lacks fallback. | One-way typed proposal interface; separate constraints; controller-independent tests; fail-safe hold/rejection. |
| R-010 | Class imbalance makes warning metrics or apparent accuracy misleading. | HIGH | MEDIUM | OPEN | High accuracy with low recall/PR-AUC or unstable false alarms/hour. | Use PR-AUC, event-level metrics, false alarms per simulated hour, missed-event rate, prevalence, and threshold preregistration. |
| R-011 | Results conflate measured MRR, simulated state, excursion, risk proxy, defect, or yield. | MEDIUM | HIGH | OPEN | Unqualified plots/reports or forbidden claim terms. | Canonical semantic classes; mandatory synthetic labels; claims matrix; automated wording scan; final audit. |
| R-012 | Pre-existing `.codex/agents` configuration conflicts with the user-mandated single-agent workflow. | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | WATCH | Existing untracked files advertise multi-agent operation and reference absent orchestration files. | Preserve as user work; do not invoke/modify; document non-authoritative status; request user direction only if removal becomes necessary. |
| R-013 | Unborn Git history obscures provenance and makes accidental overwrite harder to detect. | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | WATCH | No initial commit or tracked baseline. | Record initial file inventory; use narrow patches; report all changed paths; recommend a user-reviewed commit after phase boundary. |
| R-014 | Missing dependencies block routine implementation or encourage unnecessary optional packages. | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | OPEN | scikit-learn and Typer absent; install or import failure. | Minimal `pyproject.toml`; verify dependency need; request approval if network install is required; basic PoC independent of optional packages. |
| R-015 | Simulator timestep or benchmark language is misrepresented as production response capability. | LOW | HIGH | OPEN | Microsecond or real-time production claims without hardware evidence. | Timestep convergence; actual latency measurement; explicit simulation-only and non-production labels. |
| R-016 | Compound events create ambiguous root-cause labels and inflate attribution scores. | HIGH | MEDIUM | WATCH | R4 detects interval overlap, rejects same-target composition, requires ordered multilabel truth, and separates initiating from propagation labels; estimator scoring policy is not implemented. | Preserve ordered simulator truth offline only; freeze primary/multilabel scoring and UNKNOWN behavior in WP13; report compound cases separately. |
| R-017 | Hold behavior trivially reduces MRR error by halting processing while imposing unacceptable downtime. | HIGH | HIGH | OPEN | Predictive controller always holds, or reports MRR improvement without hold cost. | Report hold duration, recovery time, action magnitude, throughput proxy; preregister objective and comparison tolerances. |
| R-018 | Consumable-age features become machine/time identifiers and overstate generalization. | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | OPEN | Performance collapses across machines/time blocks or age correlates perfectly with split. | Grouped/time evaluation, ablation, drift analysis, and cautious association-only claims. |
| R-019 | Regression artifacts are treated as truth despite intentional scientific-model changes. | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | OPEN | Reference trace changes without decision record or tolerance rationale. | Store small canonical traces with config/version; require review and scientific checks before updating them. |
| R-020 | Scope expands into physical defect or yield modelling without labels. | MEDIUM | HIGH | OPEN | New scratch/dishing/yield targets or dashboard claims. | Reject through claims matrix and task review; allow only future-work data contracts with user-approved scope change. |
| R-021 | The PHM target or scaled process signals are assigned unsupported SI units. | HIGH | HIGH | OPEN | PublicMeasurementRecord rejects native/unknown source units, or PHM values enter SI simulator equations as Pa, m/s, rad/s, or m³/s. | Maintain separate native-unit and SI evidence planes; amend schema under versioned change control; require explicit conversion provenance and null canonical values when conversion is unsupported. |
| R-022 | PHM proxy-phase error, timestamp artifacts, or four extreme labels dominate virtual-metrology results. | MEDIUM | HIGH | WATCH | R2 implements source-order segmentation, centered time weighting, five proxy modes, unresolved-group flags, separate labels, and three preregistered treatments; downstream rankings may still be sensitive to proxy/gap choices. | In WP09 report proxy/gap ablations, all three label policies, missing-mode handling, stage/chronological stress, and no machine-generalization claim. |
| R-023 | The pump/UPW model violates the pump-system duty point, mass balance, or compliance-governed transient. | HIGH | HIGH | MITIGATED | R3 pump-curve residuals are below 1.2e-10 Pa, generated balance residuals below 1.0e-12 m³/s, pump-trip pressure decays monotonically, relief is explicit, and timestep refinement is monotone. | Keep R3 scientific tests and invariant stops as regression gates; re-open if coupling, parameter changes, or a new solver changes the operating-point or balance equations. |
| R-024 | Observation timestamp jitter creates non-causal online visibility. | MEDIUM | HIGH | CLOSED | R4 separates source/reported/arrival clocks, enforces arrival not before source, queues delivery, and audits 49 negative reported jitters with zero early release across 101 samples. | Re-open if a later online component bypasses queued delivery, uses reported time as availability, or accepts unaudited legacy source timing. |
| R-025 | Declarative scenarios validate but fail at replay or silently violate physical/cause policy. | MEDIUM | HIGH | MITIGATED | R4 versioned strict mappings reject unknown keys, invalid profile durations/ranges, same-target overlaps, and propagation initiators; all 14 scenarios and a fixed replay audit pass. | Keep target/profile/cause tests as regression gates; require reviewed target-contract additions and re-open on new composition semantics. |
| R-026 | The reference sag is tuned to force a positive CMP excursion despite healthy UPS ride-through. | HIGH | HIGH | MITIGATED | R3 freezes the healthy 25 percent/400 ms case as a negative control: 1.119 kPa pressure deviation and unchanged tool flow, with no CMP connection applied. | Preserve its configuration and trace; require separately declared degraded-UPS, pump-trip, hydraulic-demand, dressing-window, or compound positive events before controller tuning. |
| R-027 | Frozen Phase 1 interfaces and the implemented component APIs/configuration diverge. | HIGH | HIGH | CLOSED | R1 froze DynamicSubsystem 2.0.0; R3 retains that method contract and moves the additive signal schema/registry to 2.1.0 through recorded change control; conformance and strict-config tests pass. | Re-open on any unrecorded constructor/reset/step/schema change or missing complete default configuration. |
| R-028 | The reduced-order CMP structure or synthetic defaults are mistaken for calibrated tool/material physics. | HIGH | HIGH | WATCH | WP08 validates equations, bounds, limiting cases, energy residual, and timestep convergence, but no SI coefficient is calibrated from real CMP metrology and the default thermal-to-MRR coefficient is zero. | Keep parameter provenance exhaustive; preserve the PHM-native/SI separation; require topology/parameter sensitivity and mismatch studies; label annular output as the mandatory synthetic proxy; make no real-fab, defect, yield, or production claim. |
| R-029 | Exact energy depletion or another state-boundary operation crosses a hard invariant by floating-point residue. | LOW | HIGH | MITIGATED | WP10's exact 500 J depletion exposed a -1.49e-13 J residue after an accepted subtraction. The electrical state now projects that numerical residue to its configured minimum; exact-boundary regression, 35 adjacent plant tests, exact R3 artifact restoration, and the 154-test suite pass. | Retain hard state validation and exact-boundary tests; project only after the governing physical decision, never use clipping to hide a balance or solver error; re-open on any unexplained bound projection or energy mismatch. |

## Review cadence

Review this register at every phase checkpoint and before freezing Phase 3 objectives. Any realized risk that produces a test, build, download, schema, numerical, interface, licensing, permission, or physical-invariant failure invokes the failure policy rather than an ad-hoc mitigation.
